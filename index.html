How to play

  • Fill every cell with 🐱 or 🐶. Click a cell to cycle: empty → 🐱 → 🐶 → empty.
  • No more than 2 identical symbols may be next to each other, in a row or a column.
  • Every row and column has exactly 3 🐱 and 3 🐶.
  • = between two cells means they hold the same symbol; × means different.
  • Grey cells are given and cannot be changed. Cells that break a rule turn red.
  • The 💡 button fills in one correct cell for you. Undo takes back your last move.
